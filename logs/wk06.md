## MP2 Wish List

1. I wish I had a tool that remembers my assignment deadlines and tells me which assignments are coming up soon and what I should prioritize.

2. I wish I had a tool that remembers my daily spending and tells me how much money I have left in my weekly budget.

3. I wish I had a tool that remembers my workout sessions and tells me how many times I exercised this week and whether I reached my fitness goal.

**My Choice:** I would choose the assignment deadline tracker because it would help me stay organized with my college classes. It can save information in the browser using localStorage, so it does not need a server, database, or outside API.

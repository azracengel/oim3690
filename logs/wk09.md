## Mini Project 3 — Three Ideas

1. I wish I had a tool that looks up the weather forecast for my travel destination and helps me decide what clothes and essentials to pack for my trip.

2. I wish I had a tool that looks up the UV index and air quality in different cities and helps me decide the best time to spend time outdoors.

3. I wish I had a tool that looks up local events and activities in a city and helps me create a personalized itinerary based on my interests.
